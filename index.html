About the app

Everything your car life needs, in one place

From parking spots to plate-to-plate chats, Carmunicate turns your number plate into a direct line to the drivers around you.

01 — Dashboard

Every tool, one home screen

Chat, parking, vehicle management, REGO reminders, tutorials and the Driver IQ quiz — all one tap away from a single, uncluttered dashboard.

Quick actionsNo clutter
02 — Messaging

Chat with drivers, plate to plate

Left your lights on? Blocked someone in? Send a quick, anonymous message straight to the driver next to you — no phone numbers required.

AnonymousInstant
03 — Parking

Never forget where you parked

Drop a pin the moment you park, then filter your history by vehicle and date to find every spot you've ever left your car.

Location pinsHistory log

Auto alertsPer vehicle
